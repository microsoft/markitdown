# risk-xxxx.md

## Job of this doc

WHAT COULD GO WRONG + what we do about it. Captures one uncertainty or possible
failure, its impact, and the plan to reduce or survive it. Answers "what is the
danger?" - not a requirement, not a chosen design. A risk with no mitigation and
no contingency is just a worry; give it at least one.

## Risk

One-line name of the danger.

## Description

What can fail, and why it might.

## Trigger Conditions

What would set it off / early warning signs.

## Impact

What it costs if it happens (scope, schedule, safety, build).

## Likelihood

Low | Medium | High

## Mitigation

How to lower the chance or the impact BEFORE it happens.

## Contingency Plan

What to do AFTER it happens, if it still does.

## Owner

Who watches it.

## Related Documents

Link the decision / assumption / validation that this risk touches.
