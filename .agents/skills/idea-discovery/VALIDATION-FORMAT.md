# validation-xxxx.md

## Job of this doc

PROOF. Plan how to confirm or kill a risky claim / assumption by real evidence,
before it is baked into the design. Answers "is this actually true?" - not WHAT
(requirements) and not HOW (decisions). A validation with no clear pass/fail is
not a validation.

## Validation Objective

What this check must settle.

## Question Being Validated

The single claim under test, stated so it can come out true or false.

## Related Assumptions

The assumption(s) this proves or kills. Link them.

## Validation Method

Experiment | Prototype | Review | Simulation | Test

## Success Criteria

The observation that makes the claim TRUE.

## Failure Criteria

The observation that makes the claim FALSE. Must be distinct from success - if you
cannot state failure, the test proves nothing.

## Test Data / Conditions

Inputs, environment, fixtures. Enough for someone else to repeat it.
