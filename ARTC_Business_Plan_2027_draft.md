OUR SOLUTION
The Shadow Training Program
Every externally-led course delivered in 2027 is paired with internal ARTC staff who progress through a structured, sign-off pathway toward independent delivery.

Internal staff sit in on every external / accredited-body-led course as a formal shadow, not a bystander.
Shadows co-facilitate exercises, assessments and practical sessions under the lead trainer's supervision.
Shadows deliver full modules jointly with the external specialist, building a logged competency record.
Once signed off against the accreditation's train-the-trainer standard, the internal staff member leads the course solo.
A structured logbook tracks each shadow's progress against the accreditation's own train-the-trainer requirements — RoSPA, IOSH, Highfield, SHA, TVTC, SASO. Target: every externally-delivered course has an internal successor within two delivery cycles.
