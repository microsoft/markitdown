SECTION 14 · EXPERT RECOMMENDATION
Hybrid: Kynd Phase 1 + internal Phases 2–4
The proposal's strongest argument for itself is also the case for hybrid — after Phase 1, ARTC “can take the foundation and run it themselves.” This recommendation takes the vendor at its word.

VALUE
Vendor pricing is most defensible where differentiation is highest (P1 at 65k) and least defensible against internal benchmarks (P2's 320k bundle; P4's 600k/yr run-rate).

RISK
Buys out the single riskiest point — positioning by non-specialists — while keeping execution risk inside a gated, measurable internal process.

CAPABILITY
The existing SH/BA/MS/Designer team executes against a professional playbook (the MCD) — how durable capability is realistically built.

TIMELINE
Deliverable on current capacity in ≈7.5 months; Timeline B buys ≈4.5–5 months for SAR 110–140k — a conscious choice, not a default.

GOVERNANCE
Positioning approval, account list, media spend, lead data, and spokespersons all stay inside TSS/ARTC decision rights, with the guardrail enforceable at Gate 1.
